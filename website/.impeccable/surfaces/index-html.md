## Scope

`index.html`: the one-page marketing site. Mode: **Persuade**. The visitor (judges, hackers, prospective walkers) should understand within a viewport that silence is the signal, then try the dog, run the escalation demo and open GitHub.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dog *is* the interface. The page proves the mechanism by letting the visitor pet the dog and watch silence escalate, and it refuses the category default of a stock phone screenshot next to bullet points.

OWN-WORLD: A walk home at night. Deep navy (#0a0d1c to #141a44) under a quiet star field, with gold (#ffbe3d) as lamplight and the only warm accent. Mood colors do real jobs: sky (asking), amber (worried), red (alert), mint (safe). Cards are #151a33 with a 1px #2a3260 border and 24px radius. Gold buttons have a chunky darker lip. Headings are Bricolage Grotesque 800 and body text is Figtree.

STORY: Visitors pet the dog and feel it respond. They see why texts, panic buttons and map dots fail, follow four steps, and then watch silence turn into a friend's alert. They believe it's honest because of the fair comparison and the stated limits, then open GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: a huge "Pupdate", the tagline, the one-liner, and the "See how it works" (gold) and "View on GitHub" buttons. Right, at real phone scale: an iPhone with a Dynamic Island Live Activity, the dog on a glowing pedestal, a "Still good?" card with a countdown ring, and a "Swipe or rub to pet" hint. Stacks on mobile.

FORM: Brief-pinned; seed: none (pinned). Quoted: "Night-time and premium, not childish. Background: deep navy gradient (#0a0d1c to #141a44) with a subtle star field." / "Hero: big "Pupdate", the tagline, the one-liner, two buttons … On the right, a CSS/SVG iPhone mockup with the dog on a glowing pedestal". Signature interaction: rub to pet (wag + hearts), plus the 10-second escalation ladder handing off to the friend's phone. Motion grammar: the dog breathes, sections fade up once, and the mood transitions are the authored moment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
