Four steps. One very good dog.

Silence becomes the signal, so you never have to find a panic button.

  1. Start a walk

    Pick where you're going. Your circle can follow along, only while you walk.

  2. The dog checks in

    Every so often it tilts its head and asks, "Still good?"

  3. Pet it to say you're OK

    Rub across the dog, or tap Pet on the Lock Screen. It wags. You earn XP.

  4. Go quiet and your friends are alerted

    No pet? The dog gets worried, then scared, then sends your circle your location, your route and a summary.

Coercion-proof

A duress PIN shows a normal "all clear" but quietly alerts friends. Hold the dog for 2 seconds for a silent alert. A late pet can't cancel an alert; only a friend can close it.

Pace watch

Gemini notices running, backtracking, sudden stops or getting into a car, and checks in early with a reason. AI never decides whether an alert fires. Fixed rules do.

Safer routes

Walking routes that steer around spots your friends flagged as "avoid" or "caution".

Lock Screen + Dynamic Island

A Live Activity shows the dog's mood, a countdown and turn-by-turn directions, with a Pet button that works without unlocking.

Your circle

Friends see your walk only while you're walking. If you go quiet, they get a full-screen alert with a map, a summary and an "I've got them" button.

Your dog grows up

Puppy, then Scout Pup with a bandana, then Guardian Dog with a vest and headlamp. Plus a safe-walk streak.

Why not just share location?

Sharing your location is great. It just can't tell anyone that something's wrong.

Yes Partly No

Based on publicly documented features, November 2026. Apple Check In is genuinely good: it alerts a contact if you don't arrive or respond, and shares your location, battery and signal when it does. Life360-style apps vary, and some add SOS or crash detection. Pupdate's difference is the check-in during the walk, plus duress protection.

Your location goes on the walk. Then it stays home.

A safety app shouldn't become a tracking app. Pupdate only shares while it matters.

  • Shared only during a walk or an alertWhen the walk ends, your friends stop seeing you.
  • Gemini gets walk facts, never raw coordinatesThings like pace and stops, never where you actually are.
  • No adsNothing about your walk gets sold. The dog works for you.
